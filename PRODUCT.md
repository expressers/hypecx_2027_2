# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: decision makers and practitioners in Angolan companies (CX, marketing, commercial, operations leaders and their teams) deciding whether to attend, send a team or sponsor the next Hype CX. Secondary: brands evaluating a sponsorship, and press. They arrive mostly from LinkedIn, Instagram and WhatsApp links, very often on a phone.

## Product Purpose
Landing page for the 3rd edition (2027) of Hype CX, a Targeting event, described by Eládio as the biggest Customer Experience event in Angola. Success: visitors register interest in tickets, companies ask for corporate or sponsorship proposals, and the page makes 2027 feel unmissable before date and venue are public.

## Positioning
The first and biggest event about Customer Experience and innovation in the Angolan market. Curated content (independent curator Josemar Afonso), practical tools ("CX que se mede, gere e dá retorno"), restricted and qualified audience, curated networking. 2026 theme: "Quem entende, lidera."

## Operating Context
This is proposal 2 of two. Proposal 1 (repo hypecx_2027_1) follows a template chosen by Targeting's founder Francisco Kuvalela. Proposal 2 is Eládio's own vision: he asked for something totally disruptive, cinematic, an experience people have never seen. It reuses the colours, content and imagery of proposal 1.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack already scaffolded: Vite, React, Bun, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui, Sass. Static deploy (Render static site, like proposal 1).
- No backend: CTAs open email (hypecx@targeting.ao) or anchor links.
- Undecided for 2027: date, venue, ticket prices. Show "Data a anunciar" / "Em breve"; never invent them.
- Language: European/Angolan Portuguese.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Hype CX", logo files in public/media (logo-hypecx.svg, logo-hypecx-branco.svg).
- Colours from proposal 1: magenta #e0247a, violet #5b3fd6, near-black, light grey; the 2026 stage identity also uses yellow, blue and chevron/arrow shapes.
